Occupational Safety

Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) & Safety Measures

Protect Your Health. Prevent Occupational Lung Disease.

Essential Safety Practices
  • Respiratory Protection: N95/N100 masks and PAPR systems for effective filtration.
  • Engineering Controls: Ventilation, dust collection, and wet cutting methods.
  • Health Monitoring: Regular checkups and medical surveillance programs.

Respiratory Protection Systems

Essential equipment for effective dust filtration and worker safety.

N95 / N100 Masks

  • Standard particulate respirators for high-efficiency filtration.
  • Essential for filtering out fine silica and stone dust particles.
  • Requires proper fit testing to ensure an airtight seal on the face.
  • Must be replaced regularly or when breathing becomes difficult.

PAPR Systems

  • Powered Air-Purifying Respirators with positive-pressure airflow.
  • Ideal for workers with facial hair or extended exposure times.
  • Uses a battery-powered blower to force filtered air into a hood.
  • Provides a higher Assigned Protection Factor (APF) than standard masks.

Supplied Air Respirator

  • Clean air from an external source.
  • Connected to a compressor or air line.
  • Suitable for the highest-risk environments.

Full Body Protection

  • Protective suits or coveralls.
  • Gloves and eye protection included.
  • Sealed seams for added safety.

Use Engineering Controls

Effective methods to reduce dust at the source:

  • Install local exhaust ventilation.
  • Use dust collection systems.
  • Maintain equipment properly.
  • Upgrade to less dusty methods.

Dust Suppression Methods

Practical techniques to prevent dust from becoming airborne:

  • Wet cutting and grinding.
  • Water spraying systems.
  • Enclosures around work areas.
  • Regular cleaning and vacuuming.

Safe Work Practices

Guidelines for maintaining a protective work environment.

Work Schedule Management

  • Rotate workers from high-dust areas.
  • Limit exposure duration.
  • Schedule breaks in clean areas.
  • Avoid peak dust hours.

Housekeeping Standards

  • Clean with HEPA-filtered vacuums.
  • Wet mopping instead of dry sweeping.
  • Remove accumulated dust regularly.
  • Dispose of waste safely.

Regular Health Checkups

Early detection saves lives.

  • Baseline medical exam before starting dusty work.
  • Annual chest X-rays and spirometry tests.
  • Clinical monitoring if symptoms develop.
  • Early detection prevents long-term damage.

Employer Responsibilities

Create a safe workplace by providing PPE, training, and dust monitoring.

  • Supply PPE at no cost.
  • Conduct regular dust monitoring.
  • Maintain ventilation and dust control equipment.
  • Keep workers informed of hazards and safety measures.

Your Health is Non-Negotiable

Implement these precautionary measures today to protect yourself and your workforce tomorrow.
