This announcement presents mandatory quarry rules and regulations. All quarry operators must comply with applicable Indian laws and state rules without exception. Operations must be licensed under the Mines and Minerals (Development & Regulation) Act and follow the Mines Act and state quarry regulations for safe extraction, blasting, and reporting.

Environmental clearances and consents under the Environment (Protection) Act, the Air Act, and the Water Act are required where applicable. Ensure permits and consents are obtained and displayed on-site.

Dust control is mandatory. Implement engineering controls, water sprays, enclosures, and continuous monitoring to meet CPCB and State Pollution Control Board standards for particulate emissions and ambient PM10 and PM2.5 levels. Maintain records of monitoring and corrective actions.

Worker safety is essential. Provide and enforce PPE, deliver regular site safety training and inductions, maintain safe blasting protocols and exclusion zones, post safety signage, and ensure emergency response and first-aid readiness. Supervisors and contractors share responsibility for compliance.

Environmental protection measures must include sediment and runoff control, wastewater management, dust suppression, progressive rehabilitation of worked-out areas, and protection of neighboring habitats. Maintain and disclose periodic environmental monitoring reports.

Accountability is mandatory: keep complete operational, safety, and environmental records; allow inspections by authorities; report incidents promptly; and correct non-compliance immediately. Non-adherence may attract enforcement action, fines, or closure under Indian law.

For precise numeric emission thresholds, permit conditions, and legal interpretations, consult the latest CPCB and SPCB notifications and qualified legal or environmental consultants.
