Key Regulations

  • Mines & Minerals (Development & Regulation) Act, 1957 — licensing, lease conditions, reporting.
  • Mines Act, 1952 & State Quarry Rules — worker safety, blasting controls, inspections.
  • Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 & EIA notifications — environmental clearances where applicable.
  • Air (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act, 1981 & Water Act, 1974 — obtain consents from SPCB/CPCB.
  • Central/State Pollution Control Board Standards — dust emission limits, monitoring.
  • Factories Act, 1948 (where applicable) — workplace safety provisions.
  • Site Restoration — progressive rehabilitation and biodiversity safeguards.

Dust Emission Limits

Follow CPCB/SPCB standards for point-source and ambient particulate matter (PM10 / PM2.5). Implement engineering controls, water sprays, enclosures, and continuous monitoring. Verify latest numeric limits from CPCB/SPCB notifications.

Worker Safety (Checklist)

  • PPE mandatory (helmets, masks, eyewear, boots)
  • Training & induction before site access
  • Safe blasting distances, licensed blasters, permits
  • Signage, traffic management, exclusion zones
  • Emergency response, first-aid, medical facilities

Environmental Measures

  • Sediment and runoff control; silt traps
  • Stormwater & wastewater management
  • Dust suppression and green buffer zones
  • Progressive rehabilitation of worked areas
  • Regular monitoring & public disclosure
Non-compliance may attract penalties under Indian law. Maintain records, audits, and visible compliance signage.
